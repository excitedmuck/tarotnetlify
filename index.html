Questions

Before You Draw

Is the tarot reading really free?

Yes. No account, no email, no card, no credits. The whole deck runs on this page. If it helps you, there is a tip jar below, and that is the only ask.

Do I need to sign up?

No. There is no login anywhere on this site and nothing to subscribe to. Type your question, pick a spread, draw.

Where does my question go, and who can see my readings?

Nowhere, and nobody. The deck is shuffled in your browser and the reading is written there too. Your question is never sent to a server, and saved readings sit in this browser’s local storage on your own device. Clear your site data and they return to the ether.

Which spreads can I draw?

Three. The three-card spread for past, present and future. The five-card elemental spread for fire, water, air, earth and spirit. The ten-card Celtic Cross for a question that needs the long answer.

Does tarot actually predict the future?

Not here. The cards are read as a Jungian mirror: archetypes that give your own situation a shape you can look at. The reading tells you what you were already circling. What you do about it is yours.
